## Hoboken PATH Trains

Real-time PATH train arrival information for Home Assistant.

### Features

- 🚂 Real-time train arrivals from Port Authority of NY & NJ
- 📍 Sensors organized by destination station
- ⏱️ Next train arrival times with countdown
- 🔄 Updates every 15 seconds

### Configuration

After installation:

1. Go to **Settings** → **Devices & Services**
2. Click **+ Add Integration**
3. Search for **Hoboken PATH Trains**
4. Complete the setup

### Supported Stations

Newark, Harrison, Journal Square, Grove Street, Newport, Exchange Place, World Trade Center, Christopher Street, 9th Street, 14th Street, 23rd Street, 33rd Street, and Hoboken.
